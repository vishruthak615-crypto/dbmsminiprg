Tourist Medicare Assist helps travelers find healthcare support, nearby hospitals, emergency assistance, first aid guidance and medical services anytime during their journey.

One-tap SOS feature that quickly displays nearby hospitals, travel time, estimated treatment costs, emergency contacts and first-aid guidance.

📍 Location-Based Services

Uses your location to find nearby hospitals, doctors, pharmacies and emergency services.

⚡ Offline Emergency Support

Works even in weak-network tourist areas using stored emergency contacts and hospital information.

🌐 Multi-Language Support

Supports multiple languages for both domestic and international tourists.

🩺 Healthcare Services

Nearby doctors, hospitals, medical tips, emergency guidance, health records and healthcare assistance.

👤 Personal Health Records

Store blood group, allergies, emergency contacts, medical history and hospital visit records.

🗣 Emergency Translation Assistant

Provides emergency medical phrases in different languages to help tourists communicate during critical situations.
